Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

In short: Stowly does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. We have no servers. Everything the app does happens on your device, or directly between your device and services you choose to connect.

This policy explains how the Stowly Android app ("Stowly", "the app", "we") handles information. It applies to the app with package name com.stowly.app.

1. Information we collect

None. Stowly has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting service. We do not run any server that your data could be sent to, so we never receive your files, your contacts, your location or anything else about you.

2. Data that stays on your device

To work, the app keeps some information locally on your phone only:

  • Your settings and preferences.
  • The list of storage connections you set up (for example a Google Drive account or a home server).
  • Sign-in tokens and passwords for those connections. These are stored encrypted using Android's secure key storage and never leave your device except to sign in to the service they belong to.
  • Small caches such as thumbnails, search indexes and the app's trash folder.

Uninstalling Stowly, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all of this.

3. Google user data

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Stowly asks Google for permission to access your Drive files. Stowly uses this access only to show, open, upload, download, copy, move, rename and delete files that you ask it to, and to run backups or syncs that you set up yourself.

  • File contents and file details travel directly between your device and Google's servers. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your Google sign-in token is stored encrypted on your device only.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not transfer it to any third party.
  • No human at Stowly can read your Google data.
  • You can disconnect at any time by removing the connection in Stowly, and you can revoke access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Stowly's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Other services you connect

The same applies to OneDrive, Dropbox, Box, pCloud, S3 storage and network servers (SMB, FTP, SFTP, WebDAV). Stowly talks to them directly from your device, only when you ask it to. Those services have their own privacy policies, which apply to the files you keep with them.

5. Phone permissions

Stowly asks for some Android permissions so its features work. All of them are used on your device only:

  • Files and media / all files access: to browse and manage the files on your phone.
  • Photo location: to show where a photo was taken when you view its details. This is read from the photo itself and is not sent anywhere.
  • Internet and local network: to reach the cloud drives and home servers you connect, and for optional Wi-Fi file sharing to devices on your own network.
  • Installed apps and usage access: for the app manager and storage analysis screens.
  • Fingerprint / face unlock: to lock the app or the private vault. Stowly never sees your biometric data; Android handles it.
  • Notifications and background work: to show progress of copies, backups and syncs.

6. Children

Stowly is a general-purpose tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact
